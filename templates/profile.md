# Repository/profile content template

Publish as `kind: profile` with a safe slug such as `repository-profile`, contributing `source_paths`, and `review_status: draft`.

**Summary:** a modest statement of the repository's evidenced purpose.

- **Purpose hypotheses:** claim, supporting source, contrary evidence, and uncertainty.
- **Components and capabilities:** distinct areas, their roles, and selected analysis lenses.
- **Representative paths:** input/trigger to declared result, with supporting flow references.
- **Practices and standards:** supplied requirements versus observed conventions.
- **Coverage and unknowns:** inspected scope, unsupported sources, unresolved relationships, and external boundaries.

Avoid undocumented organization names, ownership, business intent, or deployment claims. A repository can have multiple purposes and mixed capabilities.
